Quick rule of thumb — predicting bond type

Compare electronegativity (EN) difference between two atoms:

ΔEN ≈ 0 – 0.4Nonpolar covalent
ΔEN ≈ 0.4 – 1.7Polar covalent
ΔEN ≳ 1.7Ionic

Metals + nonmetals → often ionic · Nonmetal + nonmetal → covalent · Metal + metal → metallic

Why this matters

The Bohr model (shells) is what this periodic table uses for teaching — clear and useful for bonding & valence. The modern quantum model is more accurate: electrons exist as probability clouds (orbitals), not fixed paths. Rutherford proved the nucleus; Thomson discovered the electron; Dalton started the atomic idea.
